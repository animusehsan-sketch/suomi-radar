Slide 1 – Title Slide
	Taxora
	AI-powered tax deduction assistant for Finland

Slide 2 – Executive Summary
	Taxora is an AI agent-powered tax assistance platform.
	Designed to simplify tax return preparation for individuals.
	Autonomously interprets tax regulations and identifies missing deductions.
	Bridges the gap between Finland’s digital tax infrastructure and personalized support needs.

Slide 3 – Problem Statement
	Finland’s tax system requires independent review of pre-filled returns and self-identified deductions.
	Extremely complex for freelancers, immigrants, remote workers, and micro-entrepreneurs.
	Language barriers and complex terminology cause significant friction.
	Users frequently miss eligible deductions (e.g., home office, YEL pension, travel allowances).

Slide 4 – Proposed Solution
	Acts as an intelligent interpretation layer over existing public systems like MyTax.
	Utilizes a conversational AI interface for natural language interaction.
	Automates the discovery of eligible deductions using financial data analysis.
	Consistently aligns recommendations with official Finnish Tax Administration guidance.

Slide 5 – Technology Architecture
	Uses a modular, multi-agent AI architecture instead of a single monolithic model.
	Orchestrator Agent: Coordinates overall workflow and communication.
	Tax Knowledge Agent: Interprets official Finnish tax regulations.
	Deduction Discovery Agent: Analyzes expenses to find eligible deductions.
	Receipt Processing Agent: Employs OCR to extract structured financial data.
	Compliance Verification Agent: Ensures suggestions stay within legal limits.
	Tax Report Generation Agent: Compiles structured, MyTax-ready summaries.

Slide 6 – Key Features
	AI Tax Chat: Conversational guidance in natural language.
	Automatic Deduction Discovery: Proactive identification of eligible expense categories.
	Receipt Scanner: OCR technology for automated data extraction and categorization.
	Tax-Ready Summary Reports: Structured outputs aligned with Finnish reporting requirements.

Slide 7 – Value Proposition
	Reduces administrative complexity compared to manual MyTax filing.
	Delivers personalized tax interpretation without the high cost of traditional accountants.
	Demystifies complex tax rules for diverse users, especially immigrants and freelancers.
	Shifts the focus from reactive compliance monitoring to proactive taxpayer assistance.

Slide 8 – Finland Market Justification
	Advanced digital infrastructure with over 90% digital tax portal usage.
	High tax morale and a low VAT gap (~3%), indicating strong overall compliance.
	Government authorities are already receptive to AI (e.g., Virtanen chatbot).
	Strict privacy rules (GDPR) align perfectly with Taxora’s compliance-first architecture.

Slide 9 – Target Market
	Freelancers & Self-Employed: Require assistance with YEL obligations and expense tracking.
	Immigrants: Need help overcoming language barriers and unfamiliar tax frameworks.
	Remote Workers: Seek clarity on home office and equipment deductions.
	Micro-Entrepreneurs: Need an affordable alternative to professional accounting services.

Slide 10 – Market Size & Opportunity
	Finland’s active workforce comprises approximately 2.7 million individuals.
	Primary early adopter segment includes 200,000–250,000 freelancers and self-employed professionals.
	Growing immigrant population of over 500,000 residents adds significant potential.
	Projected initial reach of 20,000–50,000 active users in Finland.
	High potential for geographic expansion across Nordic markets (Sweden, Denmark, Norway).

Slide 11 – Business Model
	Freemium SaaS: Free tier includes basic AI chat and limited deduction guidance.
	Premium Subscription: €5–€10/month (or €30–€50/year) unlocks OCR and advanced optimization.
	API Partnerships (B2B): Integration with accounting firms, freelancer platforms, and payroll tools.
	White-Label Licensing: Offering localized AI tax assistance for digital finance ecosystems.

Slide 12 – Competitive Advantage
	End-to-End Workflow: Unifies discovery, compliance verification, and reporting.
	Multi-Agent Architecture: Highly coordinated AI offers superior accuracy over simple chatbots (e.g., Taxxa.ai).
	High Accessibility: Specifically designed to support non-expert and immigrant users.
	Assistance-First Approach: Focuses on actively finding deductions, not just processing submitted forms.

Slide 13 – MVP Strategy
	Objective: Validate core capabilities with minimal initial development complexity.
	Scope: Web-based platform featuring AI tax chat, deduction discovery, and structured summaries.
	Early Testing: Focuses on direct feedback from freelancer and immigrant communities.
	Future Iterations: Mobile support, banking APIs, and expanded automation workflows.

Slide 14 – Financial Projection & Break-even
	Startup Costs: €14,500 – €30,000 for MVP development and initial launch.
	Year 1 (Validation): 300–600 paying users, earning €12,000 – €24,000.
	Year 2 (Break-even): 2,000–5,000 paying users, earning €80,000 – €200,000.
	Year 3 (Scalable Growth): 10,000+ paying users, targeting €400,000 – €750,000 before Nordic expansion.

Slide 15 – Risk & Compliance Strategy
	Data Privacy (GDPR): Compliance-first design with minimal retention of personal financial data.
	EU AI Act Readiness: Employs explainable AI logic to maintain transparent decision support.
	Guidance Positioning: Taxora advises, but final MyTax submission remains safely under user control.
	System Resilience: Modular design allows for simple swapping of external OCR or API services.

Slide 16 – Roadmap & Strategic Impact
	Phase 1 (MVP Validation): Develop core web application and conduct early adopter testing (Months 1–9).
	Phase 2 (Automation Focus): Launch OCR receipt processing and prepare B2B API integrations (Months 10–12).
	Phase 3 (Nordic Scaling): Adapt regulatory knowledge modules for Sweden, Estonia, Denmark, and Norway (Months 13–18).
	Long-Term Vision: Establish Taxora as the foundational AI infrastructure for personal tax navigation across Northern Europe.
